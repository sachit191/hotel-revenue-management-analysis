Hotel Revenue Management & Demand Analysis

Tools: Microsoft Excel | PivotTables | PivotCharts | Data Cleaning | Dashboard Analysis
Dataset: Hotel Booking Demand
Period covered: 2015–2017
Records analysed: 119,390 bookings
Project status: Completed – Descriptive Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses hotel booking data to identify booking patterns, cancellation behaviour, distribution channel contributions and differences in Average Daily Rate (ADR) between City Hotels and Resort Hotels.

The objective is to demonstrate how historical booking data can support revenue management decisions through structured data analysis and interactive Excel dashboards.

Business Objectives

Analyse booking demand across different months.

Compare cancellation rates between hotel types.

Evaluate the contribution of different distribution channels.

Compare Average Daily Rate (ADR) across hotel types.

Present key business findings through an Excel dashboard.

Tools and Techniques

Microsoft Excel

Excel Tables

PivotTables and PivotCharts

Data summarisation and aggregation

KPI reporting

Dashboard visualisation

Key Findings

Booking Volume: The dataset contains 119,390 booking records.

Cancellation Behaviour: Overall cancellation rate is 37.04%.

Hotel Comparison: City Hotel cancellation rate is 41.73%, compared with 27.76% for Resort Hotel.

Monthly Demand: August records the highest aggregated monthly booking count, with 13,877 bookings.

Distribution Channels: TA/TO contributes 97,870 bookings, making it the largest distribution channel.

ADR Comparison: City Hotel has an average ADR of 105.30, compared with 94.95 for Resort Hotel.

Business Relevance

The analysis highlights opportunities for hotel revenue management teams to:

Investigate higher cancellation levels at City Hotels.

Understand seasonal booking demand.

Evaluate reliance on different distribution channels.

Compare pricing performance across hotel categories.

Use historical booking patterns to inform future commercial decisions.

Data Limitations

The dataset does not provide sufficient room-inventory information to calculate actual occupancy.

Actual realised revenue and RevPAR cannot be reliably calculated from the available fields.

ADR is analysed as provided in the dataset; its currency is not specified.

This project focuses on historical descriptive analysis and does not claim to provide a predictive forecasting model.

Project Outcome

The project demonstrates practical application of Excel-based business analytics to hotel booking data, converting raw records into structured insights and management-oriented visualisations.
